## Standing Rule

Write for the person using FERN. Keep internal programme status, implementation dependencies and unresolved organisational decisions in controlled project documentation.

- Remove internal clarification requests and P0–P5 development language from ordinary content.
- Explain what users can understand or do. Do not claim unavailable workflows exist.
- Do not invent ownership, authority, cadence, system connections, records or approvals.
- Show limitations where they affect interpretation or the next step: no records available, applicability not confirmed, evidence required, specialist review required.
- Keep relevant versions, source status, applicability and evidence limitations accessible. Do not hide proposed classifications, unverified results or unavailable capabilities merely to make a page appear complete.
- Preserve detailed dependencies and validation history in the PRD and traceability documentation. This local file has not been published to Amazon's Programme Hub or P5 system.
